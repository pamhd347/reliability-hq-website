## Domain Strategy Tips

1. **Check .com first** – still the default for business credibility
2. **.io is acceptable** for technical/engineering audiences
3. **.academy is excellent** for course-focused brands
4. **Avoid hyphens** – hard to say aloud, look spammy
5. **Buy multiple TLDs** for your chosen name to protect brand
6. **Check social handles** – @gearwise, @rcmacademy, etc.
